## Abstract (own words)

They release a large open **line-image + transcription** ground-truth set for historical OCR: German Fraktur and Early Modern Latin, from 15th‑c. incunabula through 19th‑c. Fraktur books. Form is deliberately engine-friendly (line pairs for LSTM OCR: Tesseract 4, OCRopus). They also ship some pretrained OCRopus models, a Perl harmonizer for divergent transcription rules, and guidance that **OCR GT ≠ purely linguistic diplomatic transcription**.

## Scale & design

- **313,173** line pairs  
- Wide chronological span (incunabula → 19th c. Fraktur)  
- DE + Early Modern Latin  
- GT as **line crops + text** (not full-page layout GT)

## Reported model ballpark (paper claims)

Pretrained OCRopus models on subcorpora:

- ~**95%** character accuracy — early printings  
- ~**98%** character accuracy — 19th‑c. Fraktur  

(Character accuracy = 1 − CER; treat as 2018 OCRopus-era numbers on their splits.)

## Strengths

1. **Open, citable, large** — rare for historical DE OCR  
2. **Right unit of training** — line pairs match modern CRNN/LSTM OCR  
3. **Transcription-policy honesty** — OCR needs consistent glyph rules (ſ, umlauts, abbreviations) more than pure linguistics  
4. Direct lineage to Tesseract 4 / OCRopus training culture that still underlies `frk` + research engines  
5. Authors overlap with chreul models + archiscribe (one ecosystem)

## Caveats

1. **2018 stack** — OCRopus pretrained weights may not drop into Calamari 2 / current Tesseract without conversion  
2. Paper describes GT + baseline models; **not** a full production pipeline (no PDF routing, no plate handling)  
3. Mixed centuries: a model trained on all GT may dilute pure 19th‑c. craft-book performance (or help generalization — evaluate, don’t assume)  
4. Line GT assumes **already segmented lines** — UnbrokenOCR’s hard problem is often page → lines on dirty Google scans  
5. No guarantee GT covers **technical craft vocabulary** (Dachdecker-style) — mostly general historical print  
6. CC-BY requires attribution when redistributing GT

## UnbrokenOCR implications

| Keep doing | Consider adopting | Don’t blindly do |
|------------|-------------------|------------------|
| Quality-gate before index | Cite GT4HistOCR in ATTRIBUTION / research notes | Replace tesseract `frk` overnight with unconverted 2018 OCRopus weights |
| Line-aware thinking for future eval | Optional **CER eval harness** against held-out GT lines | Treat 98% paper CER as expected on Dachdecker Google scans |
| Transcription consistency (ſ sidecar) | Harmonize transcription rules if we build private GT | Confuse linguistic edition standards with OCR GT |
