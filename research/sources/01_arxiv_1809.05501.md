## Abstract (own words)

Open **line-image + transcription** GT for historical OCR: German Fraktur and Early Modern Latin, incunabula through 19th‑c. Fraktur. Form is deliberately train-ready for RNN/LSTM OCR. Ships pretrained OCRopus models on subcorpora (~95% early / ~98% 19th‑c. Fraktur character accuracy on their unseen tests), a **Perl harmonizer** for divergent transcription guidelines, and an argument that **OCR GT ≠ pure linguistic edition**.

## Method notes (paper)

- Manual transcription + philologist correction inside existing projects  
- Line images via **OCRopus segmentation**, except DTA19 → **ABBYY** line segmentation  
- Guidelines differ per subcorpus → authors **did not** blindly merge all eras; users remix by year/title dirs  
- Mixed models generalize better than single-book models (incunabula leave-one-book ~95.4% avg; combined train higher)  
- Artificial font lines < real printed lines for training quality (theme shared with 1810.03436)
